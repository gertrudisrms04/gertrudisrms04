### 👩‍💻 About

I build and evaluate machine learning and deep learning models across **speech, text, and tabular data** — most of it Indonesian-language, where public datasets and ready-made tooling are limited.

My interest isn't a better score on its own, but the reasoning behind each result. The project I learned the most from was a food price regressor that reached an R² of **0.98** — until I traced it back to a random train/test split on time-series data. I rebuilt it with a chronological split and reported the honest, much lower number.

> **Build. Test. Understand. Repeat.**

🎯 Open to **data & machine learning internships**, research collaboration, and freelance analysis.
